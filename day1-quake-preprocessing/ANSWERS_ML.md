## 4. Leakage check

The configured leaky columns are `title`, `sig`, `mmi`, `cdi`, `felt`, and `alert`. They are removed because they can directly encode or be derived from the earthquake's magnitude or post-event reporting, information that may not be available at prediction time. To perform the requested experiment, temporarily remove one column from `config.LEAKY`, rerun ETL and training, record the changed score, then restore the column to the leakage list. If the score improves after retaining a leaky column, that number is misleading because the model is using information unavailable at the intended prediction time. Do not submit the intentionally leaky version as the final model.
